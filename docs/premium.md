# Premium periods and payroll

Finance has Calculation, Orders and Employees views sharing a calculation period. CDEK eligibility requires exactly one positive remittance; the period uses its paymentDate. Commission uses collectedCents (customer payment), not amountCents (net remittance). Moscow courier/post use the recorded receipt amount and Moscow receipt date.

Nominal paydays are the 15th and next month's 1st. Cutoff leaves four full working days strictly between cutoff and nominal payday. The actual payday moves to the preceding working day without changing that cutoff. Example: October period 2 cutoff October 26, nominal November 1, actual October 30; next period begins October 27.

Calendar snapshots: https://xmlcalendar.ru/data/ru/2025/calendar.json, https://xmlcalendar.ru/data/ru/2026/calendar.json, https://xmlcalendar.ru/data/ru/2027/calendar.json. Asterisk marks a working shortened day, including transferred working Saturdays. Government 2027 transfer decision: http://government.ru/docs/59913/. The catalog supports periods whose full boundaries and paydays are covered; unsupported dates are shown separately, never guessed. Update snapshots before adding later years.

Draft adjustments are saved in settings by period and employee with revision checks. Operator rates update employees.bonus in the same transaction; salary part, vacation, official part, debt/advance, penalty, extra and SZ stay in the selected period. Operator salary defaults to zero until the period amount is entered; the general profile salary is not silently charged in full twice per month. Official part can only be changed by admin. Department heads can edit only their own operators and their own leader adjustments; operators are read-only.

Positive totals round to nearest RUB 50, negative totals remain exact and never reduce other people's payouts. UDL second-half preview requires an explicit first-half percentage bonus. The period-specific bonus from the old program is not inferred from an incomplete historical order set.

The ledger is stored separately in settings/premium-ledger. Initial import is an audited, one-off migration: payroll rows and all source order IDs are retained, missing CRM identities remain explicitly unmatched, and matched orders receive importedPayment evidence with source hash and recordedAt (not a fabricated payment date or CDEK registry). An imported open period uses the source membership and amounts, not the current receipt preview. It may be edited through scoped period adjustments until frozen.

Administrator freezes the current period into immutable rows/order membership/signer IDs. All required department heads sign that frozen snapshot. Administrator can confirm actual payment only after all signatures; confirmation archives it and opens the next period atomically. Confirmation atomically records one ЗП spend per department head for the frozen Н payouts of that head and operators; СЗ is excluded. Cash owners come from frozen signers. Insufficient balance rolls back all spends and the period transition. Stable period/head operation IDs and ledger CAS prevent repeated debits. Current signer selection requires exactly one department head with leaderPremium in each represented department, excluding partners without premium. New departments or ambiguous heads require explicit signer configuration before use; fixation fails rather than selecting arbitrarily.

The CRM response derives premiumStage; operational order status remains redeemed. Open active membership is accrued. Frozen membership never expands. After payment its orders are paid; next-period receipts and newly learned backdated receipts become accrued, excluding every previously assigned order by its stable LV/CRM key. Existing older receipts seen at fixation do not silently carry forward. Other periods remain in the normal paid-by-customer queue until active. Receipt conflicts prevent fixation. Previously paid LV reconciliation remains required before fixing a non-imported LV period. No prior-paid history is fabricated from delivery status.

CAS protects ledger revisions plus employee/order versions and period adjustment values. Frozen/paid adjustments are rejected at read and write boundaries. Snapshot fields and unmatched rows are scoped by department/operator before API output. Signatures cannot be submitted by operators or on behalf of another leader. Source Excel signatures are retained as source context only; they are not copied as CRM signatures.

Negative final balances carry exactly once into next-period debt/advance, added to any existing draft, in the same transaction as payment and cash spends. A reusable browser Excel uploader remains outside this change; the supplied initial workbook is imported through the audited migration. Cash deductions occur only at administrator payment confirmation, never at freeze or signature.

Operator cards store `sz`. Open calculations use this setting for the Н/СЗ payout columns; saved adjustments from older versions fall back to their period flag until the card is initialized. Editing СЗ in Premium also updates the employee card. Frozen and paid snapshots retain their original flag even when the card changes later.

Orders → Начислено offers a read-only next-payment switch. The API projects the next period's eligible membership using the same rollover rule, including newly learned backdated receipts after fixation and excluding assigned/paid orders. Operator/head order visibility still applies. This preview never changes the stored ledger or accrued/paid stages.
Operators see the active period's own payroll payout (including all adjustments) beside the order count. In next-period preview this changes to a percentage-only estimate from confirmed customer-paid gross amounts, with the same 50-ruble rounding; current salary and deductions never carry into the estimate. Search only filters the order list, not the period payout.

Admin can reopen only the active frozen/unpaid period. All signatures reset; the old signed rows and signatures are retained in return history. Membership and known-receipt baseline stay fixed, edits are recalculated and must be frozen/signed again. Paid periods cannot reopen.
Finance payroll statistics show each month’s two periods and their total including official amounts, with explicit preliminary/frozen/paid labels. Department heads receive only their department on the server; operators cannot access this report. Operator order volume excludes CDEK fees and is highlighted below RUB 300,000; head payouts are included without counting their operators’ orders twice.
